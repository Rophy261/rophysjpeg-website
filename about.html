My story

From the field to the sideline

Sports have always been home for me. I played through college, and that time on the field shapes how I work today: I know how a game moves, where the big moments happen, and when to give players their space.

Now I’m behind the camera, capturing that same energy for the people and brands who live it. That has meant filming pitchside and in the locker rooms for Concacaf at the Gold Cup, photographing Sergio Agüero for Manchester City and PUMA, covering the FIFA World Cup 2026, and documenting community football in Jamaica.

Whether it’s a single shoot or ongoing content, I bring an athlete’s work ethic and a storyteller’s eye to every project.
